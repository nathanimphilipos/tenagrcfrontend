# TenaGRC

**TenaGRC** is a lightweight GRC automation platform designed to help startups and growing organizations quickly understand their security and risk posture — without the friction of traditional GRC tools.

Built by a GRC Analyst for real-world teams, TenaGRC focuses on speed, clarity, and practicality.

🌐 Live site: https://tenagrc.com

---

## 🚀 What is TenaGRC?

TenaGRC is a fast, survey-driven risk analysis tool that converts qualitative inputs into:
- Structured risk narratives
- Quantifiable risk scores
- Clear, executive-friendly dashboards

The goal is simple: **give teams rapid visibility into risk so they can make better decisions — sooner.**

TenaGRC is especially well-suited for:
- Early-stage startups
- SaaS companies preparing for SOC 2
- Teams exploring FedRAMP or NIST alignment
- Organizations that need insight *before* formal audits

---

## 🧠 Design Philosophy

Traditional GRC tools are often:
- Heavy
- Expensive
- Difficult to operationalize
- Focused on documentation over outcomes

TenaGRC takes a different approach:

- **Automation-first** – Reduce manual analysis and repetitive work
- **Risk-based** – Focus on impact and likelihood, not checklists
- **Human-centered** – Outputs are written for people, not just auditors
- **Workflow-aware** – Designed to fit how teams actually work

Compliance should support progress, not slow it down.

---

## 🔍 Key Features

- **Instant Risk Scoring**  
  Calculate enterprise risk scores in seconds from structured inputs

- **Narrative Generation**  
  Convert survey responses into clear, auditor-ready risk narratives using AI

- **Framework-Aware Logic**  
  Scoring and outputs informed by:
  - NIST 800-53 Rev. 5
  - ISO 27005
  - CIS Controls v8

- **Executive-Friendly Dashboards**  
  Clear summaries designed for leadership and decision-makers

- **Low Friction by Design**  
  No lengthy setup, no bloated configuration, no unnecessary complexity
